Before you book

Still wondering about something? Message us on Rover and we’ll get back to you quickly.

Where do you offer care?

Hudson, Ohio and the surrounding area. If you live nearby and aren’t sure, send us a message and we’ll let you know.

Can we meet before booking?

Yes. We like to meet you and your pet first so we can learn their routine, quirks and favorite spots.

Why do you book through Rover?

Rover handles scheduling and payment, and every booking is backed by RoverProtect: photo and message updates, 24/7 support and reimbursement for eligible vet care.

Do you care for cats and other pets?

Absolutely. Dogs, cats, bunnies and other critters, from brand-new puppies to seniors and pets with special needs.

How early should I book for the holidays?

As early as you can. Holiday dates fill up first, so reach out as soon as your plans are set.
